# Problem Definition

Businesses often know revenue, orders, payments, and settlements separately but cannot answer what cash is truly available or which financial risks are forming.

The core pain can be explained without mentioning AI:

> Payment operations and receivables create timing gaps between revenue, expected settlement, bank cash, and obligations. Those gaps can hide cash shortages until it is too late to act.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Reconstruct where money went from order to bank credit.
- Separate actual cash from pending, forecast, predicted, and simulated values.
- Detect settlement, refund, fee, tax, and bank-credit mismatches.
- Forecast cash over 7, 30, 60, and 90 day horizons.
- Identify receivables likely to arrive late.
- Simulate what-if scenarios before committing spend.
- Recommend safe finance actions with constraints and evidence.

## Assumptions

- Initial users operate in INR.
- Demo data is synthetic and labeled synthetic.
- No live Razorpay/bank integration is claimed until implemented.
- Recommendations are advisory only in the buildathon prototype.
