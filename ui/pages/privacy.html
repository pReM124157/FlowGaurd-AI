Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: September 2026 · Compliant with Razorpay Partner Policies & Data Protection Standards

🛡️ 1. Core Commitment & Data Minimization

FlowGuard AI provides deterministic financial telemetry, liquidity early-warning audits, and cash runway predictions for businesses. We operate on a strict Data Minimization Principle:

  • We NEVER collect or store: Raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or customer banking passwords.
  • We ONLY ingest: Transaction IDs, timestamps, amounts, settlement statuses, gateway fee breakdowns, and high-level reconciliation metadata necessary for cash-flow ledger derivation.

⚡ 2. Razorpay Integration & Webhook Handling

When you connect your Razorpay account to FlowGuard AI:

  • Cryptographic Verification: All incoming webhooks are validated using HMAC-SHA256 signatures before processing.
  • Encrypted Transport & Storage: All API credentials, OAuth tokens, and webhook payloads are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256) via Supabase Enterprise PostgreSQL.
  • Read-Only Financial Intelligence: FlowGuard only accesses payment status and settlement reports. We do not initiate debits or unapproved payouts.

🗑️ 3. Right to Erasure & 1-Click Data Purge Flow

You maintain 100% sovereign ownership over your organization's data. FlowGuard includes an instant, hard-delete capability:

  • Disconnect Source: You can disconnect your Razorpay integration at any time from the Dashboard settings.
  • Instant Data Purge: Clicking "Delete All Stored Data" triggers an atomic cascade deletion across Supabase tables (ledger_entries, webhook_events, data_sources, and tenants), permanently removing all stored financial records and cached metrics.

⚖️ 4. Financial Provenance & Zero-Fabrication Policy

To eliminate hallucinated financial statements in AI workflows:

  • Metrics labelled [ACTUAL] are strictly derived from verified cryptographic payment events.
  • User-configured parameters (e.g. baseline payroll, buffer targets) are segregated and marked [USER_DECLARED].
  • AI recommendations provided by Ask FlowGuard are deterministic and auditable back to specific ledger transaction UUIDs.
